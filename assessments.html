Assessments

Three different ways to demonstrate learning, followed by a cumulative performance task. Confirm KATC wording with your teacher.

Assessment 01 · Skills check · K and A

Vector skills quiz

1. For a = (3,−2,1) and b = (2,1,4), calculate a·b and classify the angle.

Show typed solution

a·b = 3(2)+(−2)(1)+1(4)=8. Since positive, the angle is acute.

2. Find the scalar projection of p=(2,5,−1) onto q=(1,2,2).

Show typed solution

p·q=2+10−2=10 and |q|=3. Scalar projection = (p·q)/|q| = 10/3.

3. Find a×b for a=(1,0,2), b=(2,−1,1).

Show typed solution

a×b=(0·1−2(−1), 2·2−1·1, 1(−1)−0·2)=(2,3,−1).

Assessment 02 · Reasoning challenge · T and C

Explain and verify

1. A student says (a·b)×c is a valid vector expression. Explain why it is not defined.

Show typed solution

a·b is a scalar. The cross product is defined between two vectors, so a scalar crossed with vector c is not a defined operation.

2. A student claims a×b=(2,−1,3) for a=(1,2,0), b=(0,1,2). Check it and correct the result.

Show typed solution

a×b=(2·2−0·1, 0·0−1·2, 1·1−2·0)=(4,−2,1). Check: (4,−2,1)·a=0 and (4,−2,1)·b=0.

3. Explain why compba need not equal compab.

Show typed solution

compba=(a·b)/|b|, while compab=(a·b)/|a|. The denominators are generally different.

Assessment 03 · Navigation design task · A and C

Plan a river crossing

A river is 180 m wide and flows east at 1.5 m/s. A canoe moves at 2.5 m/s relative to the water. The landing point is directly opposite the launch point. Find the heading, ground speed across the river, and crossing time. Include a labelled vector diagram and explain your components.

Show typed solution

To land directly opposite, the canoe's westward component cancels the eastward current. Let θ be the angle upstream from straight across. 2.5sinθ=1.5, so θ=sin⁻¹(0.6)≈36.9°. The across-river component is √(2.5²−1.5²)=2.0 m/s. Time=180/2.0=90 s.

Final assessment · K, T, C, A

Chapter 7 cumulative task

K = Knowledge & Understanding; T = Thinking & Inquiry; C = Communication; A = Application. Check that these labels match your teacher's definitions.

Part A · K: Let u=(2,−1,2), v=(1,3,0). Find u·v, classify the angle, and calculate u×v.

Show typed solution

u·v=2−3+0=−1, so the angle is obtuse. u×v=(−6,2,7).

Part B · T: Find a unit vector perpendicular to both p=(1,2,0) and q=(0,1,1).

Show typed solution

p×q=(2,−1,1), with magnitude √6. A unit normal is (2,−1,1)/√6; its negative is also valid.

Part C · C: Explain with a labelled sketch why a×b is perpendicular to a and b, and why |a×b| gives parallelogram area.

Show typed solution

a×b is perpendicular to the plane containing a and b. Its magnitude is |a||b|sinθ; |b|sinθ is the perpendicular height on base |a|, so this is base times height.

Part D · A: A 22 N force pulls a cart 8 m at 25° to its displacement. Find the work. Then find the torque magnitude for a 22 N force applied 0.45 m from a pivot at 25° to the lever arm.

Show typed solution

Work=22(8)cos25°≈159.4 J. Torque magnitude=0.45(22)sin25°≈4.18 N·m. The data determine the magnitude only. To specify the torque vector's spatial direction, a diagram showing the lever arm and force orientation is needed; then apply the right-hand rule.
